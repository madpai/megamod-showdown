# Open Halo behavior findings and MegaMod comparison

Classification concerns **our documented assumptions**, not source-code similarity. A 2342 Xbox finding cannot by itself confirm Trial PC behavior. All findings below target Xbox PAL debug **2342** unless a port is named. `RECONSTRUCTED SOURCE` means the bnunu reconstruction at `52659b6`, which contains both exact and fuzzy work; it is not a separate original-source archive. Confidence measures what the linked material demonstrates, including the version caveat.

## F1. Tick ownership and update order

- **Finding / classification:** [Reconstructed `game_tick`](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/game/game.c#L433-L475) orders quitting-player removal, allegiance, units, AI, player pre-update, effects, first-person weapons, game rules, HS, recorded animations, objects, player post-update, then HUD/player effects. `objects_update` dispatches object types and has a separate creation/deletion pass. [`game_frame`](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/game/game.c#L987-L1018) handles sound and scenario frame work separately. **EXPLAINS A PREVIOUS MYSTERY** candidate: same-tick input, scripted spawn and projectile side effects depend on phase. It does **not** confirm MegaMod's current order.
- **Source / target / evidence / confidence / action:** bnunu/halo-1; 2342 Xbox; **RECONSTRUCTED SOURCE**; **MEDIUM** for exact phase claim, **LOW** for Trial transfer; **ADD TEST**.
- **MegaMod relevance:** [Shared tick](../../../src/app/session_tick.c) now handles world interaction, calls `hta_game_update`, drains events, advances world entities and effects, then pumps/sends host networking. Within [`hta_game_update`](../../../src/game/game.c), units/bots, vehicles, projectiles, drops and CTF advance in a different explicit order. This is an independent design, not a contradiction to fix. The [universal port README](https://github.com/cybersecurity/halo-ce-universal/blob/13d1ae2/README.md#frame-rate) says 30 Hz simulation plus display-rate interpolation; that interpolation is **PORT IMPLEMENTATION**, not original Xbox logic. MegaMod's shared tick still accepts `dt`, so a fixed-step guarantee is not established here.

## F2. Datum handles and object lifetime

- **Finding / classification:** [Data arrays](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/memory/data.c#L111-L169) use a slot plus a 16-bit identifier; lookup checks occupancy and, when supplied, identifier equality. [`datum_new`/`datum_delete`](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/memory/data.c#L273-L342) reuse slots; [`objects_update`](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/objects/objects.c#L4122-L4240) fences active, creating and deleting objects. **CONFIRMS OUR IMPLEMENTATION DIRECTION** only at the level of stale-reference risk: dense slot alone is unsafe across reuse.
- **Source / target / evidence / confidence / action:** bnunu/halo-1; 2342 Xbox; **RECONSTRUCTED SOURCE**; **HIGH** for this model, **LOW** for a universal design claim; **ADD TEST**.
- **MegaMod relevance:** MegaMod's [X1 world entities](../../WORLD_ENTITIES.md#runtime-handles) already resolve authored IDs to slot-plus-generation handles and reject stale queued events across round reset. The [entity comparison](../ENTITY_COMPARISON.md) proposes a typed common handle for other specialized pools, and [dense-index audit](../DENSE_INDEX_HAZARDS.md) identifies wire/local ordinals. Keep authored IDs, runtime handles and network IDs separate. Halo's fixed-capacity arrays, 16-bit identifier space, optional zero-identifier lookup, object type tree and PVS activation are limitations or content-specific tradeoffs, not a target design. For future dynamic pools, validate delete/reuse, cross-kind misuse, wrap policy and deletion during iteration; do not repeat X1's already landed reset test as new work.

## F3. Player/AI/object scheduling

- **Finding / classification:** [AI update](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/ai/ai.c#L1968-L2004) calls encounters and actor update; [actors](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/ai/actors.c#L4233-L4257) can be dormant/erased and have service timers. Per-actor processing includes perception, situation, emotion, decision, action, movement, look and combat. **EXPLAINS A PREVIOUS MYSTERY** candidate: apparent AI hesitation may be budgeting or dormancy rather than a weapon timer.
- **Source / target / evidence / confidence / action:** bnunu/halo-1; 2342 Xbox; **RECONSTRUCTED SOURCE**; **MEDIUM**; **INVESTIGATE LATER**.
- **MegaMod relevance:** MegaMod bots use a simpler nav/brain loop by design. Potential generic principle: measure and expose perception/path budgets and dormant state in structured telemetry before adding behaviors. Halo encounter scripting, dialogue and squad machinery would burden the generic engine.

## F4. Physics, collision, vehicles and damage

- **Finding / classification:** Reconstructed [physics](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/physics/physics.c), [collision](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/physics/collisions.c), [vehicles](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/units/vehicles.c), [projectiles](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/items/projectiles.c) and [damage](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/objects/damage.c) expose distinct query, motion, impact and damage responsibilities. **UNKNOWN** on numerical or behavioral parity with Trial; no formulas have been transferred.
- **Source / target / evidence / confidence / action:** bnunu/halo-1; 2342 Xbox; **RECONSTRUCTED SOURCE**; **HIGH** for subsystem separation, **LOW** for Trial-specific outcome; **ADD TEST**.
- **MegaMod relevance:** The [handoff ledger](../../HANDOFF.md) openly labels invented vehicle coasting, splatter thresholds, blast/recoil push, melee/grenade timing and custom hull durability. These are suitable measurement targets. The generic principle is to separate collision evidence from damage/rule decisions and to record event order. Halo vehicle mass-point, seat and BSP complexity belongs only where a compatibility test warrants it. MegaMod's destructible multiplayer hulls are an intentional Showdown rule, not a Halo compatibility bug.

## F5. Multiplayer identity and authority

- **Finding / classification:** [Network connection](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/networking/network_connection.c) has reliable and unreliable endpoints; managers distinguish machines and players. The reconstructed [server tick update](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/networking/network_server_manager.c#L1697-L1746) distributes player updates with update number, random seed and game time; the [client checks sequence and seed](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/networking/network_client_manager.c#L1631-L1707). The 128-player fork [states explicitly](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-ce-universal/blob/f2fa457/port/linux/README.md#system-link) that all machines simulate in lockstep. **CONTRADICTS** any broad assumption that “Halo networking was host-authoritative snapshots,” but **does not contradict MegaMod's chosen design**.
- **Source / target / evidence / confidence / action:** bnunu/halo-1 + bnunu/halo-ce-universal; 2342 Xbox lineage/port; **RECONSTRUCTED SOURCE** + **PROJECT DOCUMENTATION**; **HIGH** for fork's stated model, **MEDIUM** for full original protocol details; **DOCUMENT**.
- **MegaMod relevance:** MegaMod v10 uses host-authoritative snapshots/events and content fingerprints. The examined server tick message carries player updates rather than an object-state snapshot; do not infer that all Halo messages have this shape. An [in-game add-player path](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/networking/network_server_manager.c#L1743-L1777) exists, but a general mid-match world-state late join and per-object replication ID contract remain **UNKNOWN** from this review. Object datum handles should not be mistaken for stable MegaMod network IDs. Join/late-join, event replay and content compatibility need independent MegaMod tests, not packet copying.

## F6. Halo script system

- **Finding / classification:** [HS runtime](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/hs/hs_runtime.c#L1125-L1153) stores script threads in a datum array, wakes sleeping threads by game tick, and garbage-collects object lists. Its [types](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/hs/hs.h#L13-L65) include scalar values, object/unit/vehicle/weapon references, tag references and scenario-specific enums; [scenario definitions](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/hs/hs_scenario_definitions.h) hold script/global declarations and expression roots. **CONFIRMS OUR ARCHITECTURAL ASSUMPTION** that gameplay scripts need bounded engine verbs, typed references, and tick ownership. It does not argue for HS or its value model.
- **Source / target / evidence / confidence / action:** bnunu/halo-1; 2342 Xbox; **RECONSTRUCTED SOURCE**; **MEDIUM**; **DOCUMENT**.
- **MegaMod relevance:** Future host Lua should receive safe handles and bounded APIs, preserve explicit sleep/timer state, and validate authority/phase. MegaMod's [scripting comparison](../SCRIPTING_COMPARISON.md) already reaches this independently. Avoid exposing raw C pointers or Halo object/tag types.

## F7. Rules and version boundaries

- **Finding / classification:** Separate [CTF](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/game/game_engine_ctf.c), [Slayer](https://github.com/bnunu/halo-1/blob/52659b6/source/game/game_engine_slayer.c) and other mode modules reinforce that objective/rule transitions deserve their own state tests. **BUILD DIFFERENCE — NOT DIRECTLY APPLICABLE** to Trial/Showdown timing and scoring until observed. The 2342 build's scenario, tags and game options can differ from PC Trial.
- **Source / target / evidence / confidence / action:** bnunu/halo-1; 2342 Xbox; **RECONSTRUCTED SOURCE**; **MEDIUM** for organization, **LOW** for transferable timing; **ADD TEST**.
- **MegaMod relevance:** Compare CTF pickup/drop/return/capture, respawn and score timing with Trial only where Open Halo accuracy is intended. Do not treat Showdown's imported-map CTF policy as a Halo requirement.

## What was historically guessed, and what is still unknown

MegaMod's [handoff](../../HANDOFF.md) and [journal](../../JOURNAL.md) trace tag-derived mechanics, observed phone results, reverse-engineered offsets, and an explicit invented-constant ledger. Relevant candidates include movement, spread/recoil, melee and grenade timing, pickup and respawn behavior, seat transitions, CTF flags, vehicle coasting/impulses, projectile collision and damage/shield outcomes. The reconstruction gives stronger **2342** evidence for subsystem ownership, handles and phase order. It does **not yet** establish Trial values for those mechanics, and this study found **no verified contradiction of a current Open Halo Trial behavior**. Apparent conflicts in tick order or lockstep authority are differences in product design, not defects by themselves. The [test plan](FOLLOW_UP_TESTS.md) is the next evidence step.
